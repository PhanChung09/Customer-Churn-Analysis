## Limitations and Verification

AI did not independently verify the underlying data quality, model implementation, or business assumptions. The reported recommendation depends on the notebook's results and assumptions, including:

- $5 outreach cost per contacted customer.
- $500 budget per 1,000 active customers.
- 30% retention success rate among contacted churners.
- $100 value for each retained customer.

Before operational use, the company should validate the model on an untouched holdout set and conduct a controlled retention pilot with a treatment group and a holdout control group.
